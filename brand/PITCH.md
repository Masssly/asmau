# What Asmau stands for, and how we talk about it

Owner's principles, 17 November 2026. Use them in every pitch, store listing, funding application and social post.

## Never charge for the knowledge

The games are free, all of them, for ever: no paid tier, no subscription, no account, no adverts. Knowledge of the
prophets and of the great Muslims should be open to every child. Money comes from donations, grants and, later, a
merchandise shop on the website only (never inside the store apps: `class="web-only"`, like donations).

## What sets Asmau apart (from the comparison of 17 November 2026)

Lead with the first two; they are what other Islamic children's games do not have.

1. **Free for ever, all of it, with no account.** Others ask for a subscription for most of their content, or use free
   games to sell classes or memberships.
2. **Parents read the stories in their own voice, in any language**, and the family's devices share the recordings
   (family code). No other app we found offers this.
3. **Whole stories, not snippets**: 11 to 25 chapters, every chapter tied to its Qur'an verses.
4. **Sources in the open**: commentary and hadith named; Sunni and Shia sources side by side; a note for parents on
   every page.
5. **Children see themselves**: Black African children in the prophets' stories; the Great Muslims drawn as the people
   they really were.
6. **Heroes others do not tell**: Nana Asma'u, Umm Ayman, Ibn Battuta, Rahmah El Yunusiyyah, and more to come from the
   lands Muslim families come from.
7. **Made for low data and offline**: a family chooses what to download, and the games keep working without internet.
8. **The Prophet ﷺ is never shown**: a soft breeze where he is present.
9. **Many languages, each checked by a native speaker** (Bahasa Indonesia first).

Do not claim "no sign-up" as unique: some other free apps also need none. The claim is the whole package: everything
free, no account, nothing to buy.
