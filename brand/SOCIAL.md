# Asmau on social media

The accounts, what each one says, and the launch posts. Pictures: `brand/kit/social/` (made by `brand/src/social.js`;
never hand-edit, change the script and run it). Every picture is also at `https://asmau.org/brand/kit/social/<file>`.
How we talk: brand/PITCH.md (free for ever; your own voice; whole stories with sources). Plain, warm, short, British
spelling. Never post a picture of the Prophet ﷺ (there are none) and never a child's real photo.

## Who we talk to

Parents, grandparents, teachers and mosques: not children. Children play on asmau.org; the accounts are for the
grown-ups who choose what children play.

**About (Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, WhatsApp Channel):**
Asmau is free Islamic story games for curious children: the prophets and great Muslims, told whole, chapter by
chapter, with something to do on every page.

- Free for ever: no adverts, no account, nothing to buy.
- Parents can read every story in their own voice, in any language, on all the family's devices.
- Every chapter is tied to its Qur'an verses; commentary and hadith are named, Sunni and Shia sources side by side, with a note for parents on each page.
- Children see themselves: the prophets' stories drawn with Black African families; the great Muslims drawn as the people they really were.
- Low data: download a game once and it works without internet.

Named after Nana Asma'u bint Usman dan Fodio, the scholar and teacher of 19th-century Sokoto.
Play at https://asmau.org
