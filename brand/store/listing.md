## Full description (Apple 4000, Google 4000, Huawei 8000)

Asmau is a free collection of Islamic story games for children, named after Nana Asma'u, the West African
scholar and poet who taught women and girls two hundred years ago.

STORIES OF THE PROPHETS
• Adam: made from earth, taught the names, forgiven. 11 chapters.
• Yusuf: the dream, the well, the prison and the king. 13 chapters.
• Musa: from the basket on the river to the parting of the sea and al-Khidr. 25 chapters.

GREAT MUSLIMS
• Nana Asma'u: the girl who learned the Qur'an by heart, saved her father's books and sent women teachers out to the villages. 11 chapters.

FOR LITTLE ONES
• Tap & Say: first words in English and Arabic: night and the mosque, prayer, animals in the Qur'an, good food, Allah's world, and Eid, school and prayer in West Africa.
• Count with Me: tap moons, lanterns, raindrops and dates, count out loud and say Mashallah, up to 5, 10, 15 or 20.

HOW IT WORKS
• Every page is read aloud, so children who cannot read yet can follow and play.
• A picture for every page, and little games inside every story: puzzles, sorting, matching, counting.
• The prophets' stories are told from the Qur'an, with the verses on every chapter, and from the classical
  commentaries, Sunni and Shia. A note for parents on every chapter says what comes from where.
• Parents can record the stories in their own voice, in any language, and the game plays their voice instead.

SAFE FOR CHILDREN
• Free. No adverts, no in-app purchases, no accounts.
• No tracking, no analytics, nothing collected about you or your child.
• Each game works without internet once it has been opened.

For ages 1½ to 9. Under 5, play together with a grown-up.
