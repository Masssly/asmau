# Asset Provenance and Licensing

This folder is intended to become a reusable library for Asmau. Every asset added here must have a clear origin and permitted-use record.

## Required record for each asset

- Asset ID and filename.
- Creator / artist / generator.
- Creation date.
- Source references used for cultural or historical research.
- Whether the asset is original, generated, adapted or externally sourced.
- License and permitted use.
- Attribution requirements, if any.
- Any restrictions on commercial use, modification or redistribution.
- Review status for cultural and historical accuracy.

## Generated artwork

AI-generated concept art should be labelled as such and should not be presented as documentary evidence or a historically accurate reconstruction. Record the tool and prompt/process where available. Review all generated writing, inscriptions, clothing details and architecture for errors.

## External references

Do not copy museum, photographer, artist or commercial-game images into the repository unless the license explicitly permits it. Reference links and written observations are preferable to copying protected images.

## 3D production

For Blender files, textures, fonts, audio and GLB exports, record licenses for all embedded or linked components. Ensure the repository's overall license is compatible with every included asset.

## Cultural review

Where possible, ask people knowledgeable about the represented community, language, craft or historical period to review the assets. Record feedback and corrections.
