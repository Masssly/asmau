  robe, so it may read as Arab or Maghrebi rather than Hausa-Fulani; the neck is uncovered; the face is more
  aged and smaller-featured than the other two characters, which may suit a 60s teacher but is less
  child-appealing.

  folder, writing board. Manuscript pages must show **no readable or pseudo-Arabic text**; show them closed,
  edge-on, or blank until a real, verified inscription is supplied.
