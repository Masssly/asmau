# Hausa-Fulani wardrobe brief (first pair)

Every item carries region, period and confidence. Period is **Sokoto-era inspiration (19th century and
after)**, not ancient Egypt; if used in the Yusuf world, label it internal artistic reinterpretation (see
`README.md`). No script, pseudo-script or calligraphy on any garment or cap.

## Rules

- Colour restraint: indigo, undyed cream, one rose, one green accent; follow `STYLE-GUIDE.md` palette.
- Skin: deep brown with warm highlights, never grey, never green.
- Embroidery is geometric only (triangles, blades, roundels).
