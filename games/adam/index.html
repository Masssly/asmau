The Story of Adam

Eleven chapters about the first of all people. Play a little every day.

Ages 5–9

🎙️ Parents: tell this story in your own voice, in any language ›
For parents
  • The story of Adam is told in several surahs. This game follows Surah Al-Baqarah 2:30–38, Surah Al-A‘raf 7:11–27 and Surah Ta-Ha 20:115–123, with passages from surahs 3, 4, 5, 15, 18 and 38.
  • It draws on Sunni and Shia sources: Ibn Kathir’s commentary and hadith in al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Tirmidhi and Abu Dawud; al-Tabatabai’s al-Mizan and the narrations of the Imams of Ahl al-Bayt collected in Majlisi’s Hayat al-Qulub. Where they differ (how Hawwa was made, what the tree was, how to understand Adam’s eating from it, the words of his repentance) the story uses words both accept and the chapter note gives both.
  • Told gently: the eating from the tree (the pictures show leaves, not people), and the two brothers (5:27–31), which a parent may skip.
  • Shown in this story: Prophet Adam (peace be upon him), Hawwa and their children. The angels are shown only as light with wings, and Iblis only as smoke and flame, never with a face.
