Ask: what makes a gift good, its size or the heart behind it? The gift that was accepted came from someone mindful of Allah.

You may hear why the gifts were offered. Ibn Kathir reports from several early scholars, and from Ibn Abbas (in Ibn Abi Hatim), that each of Adam's children was born a twin, a boy and a girl, and that a son married the twin sister of another son, as the first family had no one else; the brothers quarrelled over which sister each would marry, and Adam asked both to offer a gift to settle it. Ibn Kathir calls Ibn Abbas's chain 'better than good'. The Qur'an does not give the reason, and the game leaves the question out.
