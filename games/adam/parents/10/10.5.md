This page is sad on purpose. If your child is upset, hold them and talk about it: anger that is not calmed can do terrible harm. That is why we stop, breathe and make wudu when we are angry.

You may meet these reports: that Satan showed the angry brother how to kill by crushing an animal's head with a rock (Ibn Jarir, in Ibn Kathir), and that when Hawwa learned of it she wept (Ibn Abi Hatim from Zayd bin Aslam). Ibn Kathir passes them on without calling them strong; they are commentary, not Qur'an or hadith.

A hadith in al-Bukhari, Muslim and others (quoted by Ibn Kathir) says that whenever a life is taken unjustly, the first son of Adam carries a share of it, because he was the first to make killing a practice.
