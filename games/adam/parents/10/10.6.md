The verse says the raven showed the brother how to cover his brother's body, and he said 'Could I not have been like this raven?' (5:31). The game says only that the raven scratched in the ground.

In Hayat al-Qulub, Adam wept for forty days for the son he lost.

As-Suddi reports from the Companions (in Ibn Kathir) that Allah sent two ravens, one of which killed the other and buried it, and that is how the brother learned to bury. The verse itself says only that a raven scratched the ground to show him.
