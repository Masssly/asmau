Ask: what do you want to teach your own children one day? What did your grandparents teach your parents?

In Hayat al-Qulub, Adam lived 930 years (Imam al-Sadiq) or 936 (Imam al-Baqir).

You may read that Qur'an 7:190, which speaks of a couple who were given a child and then 'ascribed partners' to Allah, is about Adam and Hawwa. Ibn Kathir prefers al-Hasan al-Basri's explanation, which he says has authentic chains: the verse moves from the first couple to their descendants, and it is the idol-worshippers among their children it describes, not Adam and Hawwa.
