Iblis swore he was giving good advice (7:21). Talk about it: can someone say 'trust me' and still be lying? How can we tell?

You may meet the story that Iblis got into the Garden inside the mouth of a serpent. Ibn Kathir says early commentators such as as-Suddi, Abu al-'Aliya and Wahb bin Munabbih passed on 'Israelite reports' (isra'iliyyat) about the serpent and Iblis entering the Garden, and he notes the story comes from the Torah. The Qur'an says only that Satan whispered to them.
