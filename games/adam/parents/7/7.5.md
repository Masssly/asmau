Feeling ashamed after a mistake is normal. Talk about it gently: the feeling tells us something went wrong; the next step is to say sorry, as Adam and Hawwa will.

You may meet a report that Adam ran away in shame, his hair caught in a tree of the Garden, and Allah called: 'Adam, are you running from Me?' and he answered: 'No, out of shame before You.' Ibn Kathir calls this hadith 'strange' (gharib), with a break in its chain between Qatada and Ubayy bin Ka'b, so it is weak.
