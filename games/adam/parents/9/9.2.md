In al-Mizan, al-Tabatabai quotes a narration from al-Qummi: Adam came down on al-Safa and Hawwa on al-Marwa, the two hills pilgrims walk between in Hajj and 'Umrah.

Ask: how would you feel if you were lost and then found your family again?

You may hear different places for where Adam came down. Ibn Kathir's full Arabic commentary collects them: India (as-Suddi, al-Hasan al-Basri, and Ibn Abbas in one report), a land called Dahna between Mecca and Ta'if (Ibn Abbas in another), or al-Safa with Hawwa on al-Marwa (Ibn 'Umar). Al-Hasan's report also places Hawwa at Jeddah, Iblis near Basra and the serpent at Isfahan. As-Suddi adds that the Black Stone came down with Adam. These are reports from early scholars, not from the Qur'an, and they disagree with each other.

You may also hear how long Adam was in the Garden: Ibn Abbas, in al-Hakim (who calls it authentic by the standard of al-Bukhari and Muslim), says from the afternoon prayer to sunset; al-Hasan says an hour of the Garden's day, which is 130 years of ours. (Translations from Ibn Kathir's Arabic are ours.)
