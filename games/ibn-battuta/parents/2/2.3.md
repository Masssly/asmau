The imam Burhan al-Din the Lame: "You must certainly, if God will, visit my brother Farid al-Din in India, and my brother Rukn al-Din Zakariya in Sind, and my brother Burhan al-Din in China, and when you reach them convey to them a greeting from me" (vol. I, pp. 22-23). The book tells this as a karama, a wonder given to a holy man; the game says only what he said.
