Talk about plans: what plans does our family make? Allah's plan in this story takes many years, and it starts with a baby.

Where did Pharaoh's fear come from? Ibn Kathir (in his Arabic commentary on 28:4) says the Egyptians had heard it from the Children of Israel: a saying of Ibrahim, after a tyrant of Egypt tried to take his wife Sara, that among his descendants would be one who would bring down the king of Egypt.
