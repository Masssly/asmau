Ask: what is the bravest thing your mother or father has done for you? This is a good moment to tell your child a story from when they were a baby.

Ibn Kathir passes on what 'they mention' (dhakaru), commentary rather than Qur'an: that Pharaoh's people, afraid of losing their workers, had the boys killed one year and spared the next; Harun was born in a year of sparing and Musa in a year of killing. Musa's mother lived by the Nile; she would hide him in the chest on the water, tied with a rope, whenever someone she feared came in, and one day she forgot to tie it, and the river carried him to Pharaoh's house. (Our translation from the Arabic.)
