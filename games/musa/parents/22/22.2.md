Ask: what are you afraid of trying? Being afraid is normal; what matters is what we do next.

You may meet stories of giants in that land, above all 'Uj bin 'Anaq, said to be more than three thousand cubits tall and to have outlived the Flood. Ibn Kathir rejects them firmly: they are 'reports made up by the Children of Israel', 'something one is ashamed to mention', against the authentic hadith that Adam was sixty cubits tall and people have grown smaller since; the claim that he survived the Flood is 'a lie and a fabrication', since the Qur'an says the unbelievers were drowned; and 'whether a man called 'Uj bin 'Anaq existed at all is doubtful, and Allah knows best'.

The story that a giant scooped up Musa's twelve scouts in his sleeve with his fruit: Ibn Kathir says of its chain, 'there is doubt about it'. (Our translations from the Arabic.)
