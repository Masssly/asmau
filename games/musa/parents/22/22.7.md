Ask: what does it feel like to go round in circles? Sometimes we must wait for a long time.

You may hear what came after the forty years. Ibn Kathir reports from Ibn Abbas that Musa and Harun died during the wandering, and that Yusha' bin Nun then led the next generation into Jerusalem; as the sun was about to set on a Friday, Yusha' asked Allah to hold it back until the city was won. Ibn Kathir says there is evidence for this in the Sahih.
