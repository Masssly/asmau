Ask: what makes things grow green? Al-Khidr's name means 'the green one'.

You may hear that al-Khidr is still alive. Ibn Kathir reports that al-Nawawi and Ibn al-Salah leaned that way, but says 'none of that is authentic; the best known are the hadiths of condolence, and their chain is weak'. Other scholars held that he died, citing 21:34 ('We have not granted everlasting life to any other human being before you'), and that he never came to the Prophet ﷺ, though he would have followed him had he been alive. (Our translation.)

The meaning of his name is authentic: al-Bukhari reports the Prophet ﷺ saying he was called al-Khidr because he sat on a patch of dry, withered plants, and it stirred green behind him.
