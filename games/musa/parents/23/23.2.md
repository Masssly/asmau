Musa wanted to keep learning, even though he was a great prophet. Ask: what would you travel far to learn?

Why did Musa set out? In a hadith in al-Bukhari (quoted by Ibn Kathir), Musa was asked who was the most learned person, and he said: I am. Allah rebuked him 'because he did not refer the knowledge to Allah', and told him of a servant at the meeting of the two seas who knew what Musa did not.

Musa said he would travel 'for a long time' (huqub): Ibn Kathir reports that 'Abdullah bin 'Amr said eighty years, Mujahid seventy, and Ibn Abbas a lifetime.
