Ask: have you ever forgotten to tell someone something important? Yusha forgot too.

You may meet a report (in Ibn Jarir al-Tabari) that Musa's young helper drank from the Spring of Life, became immortal, and was set adrift in a boat until the Day of Judgement. Ibn Kathir: 'a weak chain; al-Hasan [bin 'Umara] is abandoned as a narrator, and his father is unknown'. The authentic hadiths name the helper as Yusha' bin Nun, who led the people after Musa.
