Ask: when is Allah with you? Always, everywhere.

You may meet two hadiths about Musa in al-Bukhari that surprise people. In one, the angel of death came to Musa and Musa struck him; Allah then offered Musa a year of life for every hair his hand would cover on an ox's back (al-Bukhari 1339 and 3407). In the other, Musa bathed alone and a stone ran off with his clothes, and he chased after it (in al-Bukhari's book on the Prophets; check the number on sunnah.com before quoting it). Both are in al-Bukhari; scholars explain them at length, and they are best read with a teacher.
