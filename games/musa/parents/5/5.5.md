The Qur'an does not say how Musa knew which people were his own; in 28:15 it simply calls the Israelite in the fight "one of his own party". This page, added so that page 6.1 makes sense to a child, tells what the early reports give as the background.

The commentary reports that Musa's mother would not live in the palace: she nursed him in her own home, and the queen paid her for it, so he spent his first years with his real family.

Al-Tabari's commentary reports from Ibn Abbas that once Musa was grown, none of Pharaoh's people could wrong an Israelite or force him to work while Musa was there. People put this down to his having been nursed by an Israelite woman, "unless Allah had shown Musa what He had not shown anyone else". Al-Tabari also reports, from Ibn Ishaq, that a following from the Children of Israel gathered to Musa and listened to him. These are reports, so the game says "it is told that".
