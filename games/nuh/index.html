The Story of Nuh

Eleven chapters about the prophet who never gave up, and the great Ark. Play a little every day.

Ages 5–9

🎙️ Parents: tell this story in your own voice, in any language ›
For parents
  • The story of Nuh is told in several surahs. This game follows Surah Hud 11:25–49 and Surah Nuh (71), with passages from surahs 17, 23, 29 and 54.
  • It draws on Sunni and Shia sources: Ibn Kathir’s commentary, with the words of Ibn Abbas in al-Bukhari, and the narrations of the Imams of Ahl al-Bayt collected in Majlisi’s Hayat al-Qulub. Where they differ (the son’s name, the “oven”, how long the building took) the story uses words both accept and the page notes give both.
  • Told gently: the flood and Nuh’s son (11:42–47). No picture shows anyone in the water. The dove, the raven and the rainbow are not in the Qur’an or these sources, so they are not in the game.
  • Shown in this story: Prophet Nuh (peace be upon him), his son and the believers, all with dark skin, every woman’s hair covered. The statues the people worshipped are shown as plain worn stones with no faces.
