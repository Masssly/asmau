Where the statues came from: al-Bukhari reports from Ibn Abbas (quoted by Ibn Kathir) that Wadd, Suwa', Yaghuth, Ya'uq and Nasr were named after righteous men of Nuh's people. When they died, Satan prompted their people to set up statues in their gathering places, named after them; the statues were not worshipped until that generation had died and the knowledge was lost. Ibn Abbas adds that these idols later turned up among the Arab tribes.

The Shia narrations name other idols that Nuh's people set up, and do not link them to the five names in the Qur'an.
