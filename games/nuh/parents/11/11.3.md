Al-Judi: Mujahid (in the commentary) says it is a mountain in al-Jazira, northern Mesopotamia; Qatada says the Ark rested on it for a month, and that early Muslims could still see it. The Shia narrations call it 'a great mountain in Mosul'. A Shia narration from Imam al-Sadiq says the Ark first went seven times around the Ka'ba.
