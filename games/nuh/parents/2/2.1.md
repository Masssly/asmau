Nuh's name: the Shia narrations in the Shia narrations give him an earlier name, 'Abd al-Ghaffar (Imam al-Sadiq) or Sakar (Imam al-Rida), and say he was called Nuh, from nawh, mourning, because he grieved so much over his people (or, in another narration, over himself). The commentary on these verses does not discuss the name.
