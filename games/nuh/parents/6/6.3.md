The commentary explains 'with Our revelation' as 'We will teach you what to do'.

You may hear the Ark's size: The commentary quotes Muhammad bin Ishaq, 'from the Torah', that it was of Indian teak, eighty cubits long and fifty wide, thirty high, with three decks: animals at the bottom, people in the middle, birds at the top. It comes from the People of the Book, not from the Qur'an or hadith.

The Shia narrations say the Ark was built at the place of the mosque of Kufa, and give thirty, eighty or a hundred years for the building; those narrations reject the figure of five hundred.
