'The oven gushed forth' (11:40): Ibn Abbas, in the commentary, says the 'oven' (tannur) is the face of the earth, which became springs of water, until water gushed even from the places of fire; The commentary calls this the view of most early and later scholars. The Shia narrations place it in an oven in the mosque of Kufa, which Nuh had sealed and opened when the Ark was finished. The game says only that water burst up even where fires had burned.
