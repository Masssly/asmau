The commentary: a pair of every kind of creature with a soul; 'some said' plants too. 'It has also been said' that the parrot was the first bird to board and the donkey the last animal.

You may hear a story, in the Shia narrations of Hayat al-Qulub, that the donkey stopped at the door because Iblis was holding on to it, and both came in. The game leaves this out.
