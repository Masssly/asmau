About eighty believers: Ibn Abbas in the commentary ('eighty people including their women') and Imam al-Rida in the Shia narrations ('eighty believers'). Another Shia narration, from Imam al-Baqir, says only eight believed; the book reconciles the two.

Not everyone in Nuh's family believed: The commentary names his son and his wife among those who did not board, and the Qur'an gives Nuh's wife as an example of a disbeliever married to a righteous man (66:10).
