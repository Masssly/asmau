Rahmah El Yunusiyyah was born in 1900 in Bukit Surungan, Padang Panjang, West Sumatra, then part of the Dutch East Indies. Her birthday is given differently: 26 October 1900 (Wikipedia's article on her) or 20 or 29 December 1900 (Indonesian sources), so the game gives only the year.

The notes in this game come from the histories cited in Wikipedia's article "Rahmah el Yunusiyah" (Audrey Kahin, Jeffrey Hadler's Muslims and Matriarchs, Pieternella van Doorn-Harder's Women Shaping Islam, and others) and from Fitriyanti et al., Journal of Educational Sciences 10(1), 2026.
