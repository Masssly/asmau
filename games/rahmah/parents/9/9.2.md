During the war of independence that followed, she set up supplies for the Republic's side. The Dutch held her prisoner for seven months in 1949.

In 1955 she was elected to parliament for the Masyumi party, one of the first women in it. She later supported the PRRI, a rebellion against the central government (1958), lost her seat, and was arrested in 1961, then freed under an amnesty. The game leaves her politics out.
