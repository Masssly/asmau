Tap & Say

Tap something!
Then say the Arabic word together
For parents

For ages 1½ to 4. Tap a picture to hear its name in English and then in Arabic. The voice is computer-made (Google’s text-to-speech), so it may not say every Arabic word perfectly. Say it together, or record it in your own voice and the game will play your recording instead.

“Find it!” asks for one thing at a time. A wrong tap simply says the name of what was tapped; nothing is ever marked wrong. The animals are ones the Qur'an mentions; five of them give a surah its name (the cow, the bee, the ant, the elephant, the spider).

The West African page: the ram for Tabaski (Eid al-Adha); the kettle for wudu (the buta in Hausa); the pen of the Qur'an school; the drummers who go round in Ramadan to wake people for suhur; the turban; and the sandals left at the mosque door.

The Arabic words carry full vowel marks. If you speak Arabic and spot a mistake, please tell us.
