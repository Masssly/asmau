Umm Ayman

The Story of Umm Ayman

Eleven chapters about Barakah, who looked after the Prophet ﷺ all his life. Play a little every day.

Ages 5–9

🎙️ Parents: tell her story in your own voice, in any language ›
For parents
  • Umm Ayman, whose name was Barakah, was an Abyssinian woman in the household of the Prophet’s father. She looked after the Prophet ﷺ from his birth, brought him home to Makkah when his mother died, and stayed close to him all his life. He set her free and called her “O mother”. She was the mother of Ayman and of Usamah ibn Zayd.
  • This is history, not the Qur’an. The facts come from Sahih Muslim and al-Bukhari (the words of Anas and Ibn Shihab), from Ibn Sa’d’s al-Tabaqat, and from Shia collections (al-Saduq’s al-Amali, al-Kulayni’s al-Kafi). Things only Ibn Sa’d reports are told as “it is told”. Where the sources differ (when she died; a dream before al-Husayn’s birth; Fadak) the page notes give both.
  • Told gently: the deaths of the Prophet’s mother, his grandfather and the Prophet himself. At Uhud only her care for the hurt is shown, never the fighting.
  • Shown in this story: Umm Ayman, an Abyssinian with deep dark brown skin, and the Arabs of Makkah and Madina around her, every woman’s hair covered. The Prophet ﷺ is never shown, at any age: where he is there, the picture leaves him out and a soft breeze passes over it.
