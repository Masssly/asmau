You may hear (al-Saduq, al-Amali, from Imam al-Sadiq) that Umm Ayman dreamed "one of your limbs was lying in my house", that the Prophet ﷺ said it meant Fatimah would have al-Husayn, and that she carried the newborn to him. Sunni books tell the same dream of Umm al-Fadl, the wife of al-'Abbas (Ibn Majah 3923). The game leaves both out.
