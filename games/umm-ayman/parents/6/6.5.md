Zayd ibn Haritha had been given to the Prophet ﷺ by Khadijah, and he freed him (Ibn Sa'd, vol. 8).

Umm Ayman married Zayd after her marriage to 'Ubayd ibn Zayd had ended. The sources read for this game (Ibn Sa'd, vol. 8; Ibn Shihab in Sahih Muslim 1771) do not say whether 'Ubayd died or they divorced; some retellings say he died, but give no early source. In Islam a woman is married to one husband at a time, so the game says only that her first marriage had ended.
