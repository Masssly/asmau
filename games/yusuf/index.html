The Story of Yusuf

Thirteen chapters. Something to do in every one.

Ages 5–9
For parents
  • This is a retelling in simple words of Surah Yusuf (Qur’an 12:4–101), not a translation. Every chapter was checked against M. A. S. Abdel Haleem’s English translation, and each chapter links to its own verses so you can read them together.
  • Two parts are told more gently than the verses tell them. What happened in the governor’s house (12:23–34) is told only as: someone wanted Yusuf to do wrong, he refused, and lies were told about him. The second prisoner’s dream (12:41) is told only as sad news.
  • No person is drawn anywhere in the game. Yusuf appears as a warm glow, his brothers as a huddle of dots.
  • The voice is a plain computer voice, standing in until a real one is recorded. Record your own voice and the game uses it straight away on this device.
