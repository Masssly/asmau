Yusuf

The Story of Yusuf

Thirteen chapters. Three or four things to do in every one.

Ages 5–9

For parents
  • This is a retelling in simple words of Surah Yusuf (Qur’an 12:4–101), not a translation. Every chapter was checked against M. A. S. Abdel Haleem’s English translation. Under each scene, “For parents: this chapter” says what the verses say beyond the child’s version, names whose commentary an explanation comes from, and links to the verses.
  • Some parts are told more gently than the verses tell them: what the woman of the house tried to do (12:23–25), the gathering of the women of the city (12:30–32), and how the second prisoner’s dream ends (12:41). Verses 12:52–53 are left out because scholars differ on who is speaking. Each chapter note says exactly what was left out.
  • A few details come from Ibn Kathir’s tafsir (abridged English) and not from the verses: the stone Yusuf stood on in the well, the shirt the brothers forgot to tear, the twenty coins, and the wind that carried the scent of the shirt. Each is marked on its scene, and the chapter’s note names who the report is from.
  • People are shown in this game, all with dark skin, including Prophet Yusuf and Prophet Yaqub (peace be upon them). Muslims differ on whether prophets should be shown, and this game shows them. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ does not appear.
  • The pictures were made with an AI image generator (Google Gemini). They are an artist’s-eye view in a West African style: nobody knows what these people or places looked like, and the clothes and buildings are not a reconstruction of ancient Egypt.
  • Everything reacts to a touch. Swipe a scene, or use the big buttons, to turn the page.
  • The music and sound effects are made by the browser as you play, so there are no recordings. 🎵 turns the music off on its own, and 🔊 turns off the voice and the effects. Families differ on music in children’s media, so both choices are remembered on this device.
  • English is read by a plain computer voice, standing in until a real one is recorded. Arabic is never read by a computer voice: it plays only once a parent has recorded it. Record your own voice and the game uses it straight away on this device.
