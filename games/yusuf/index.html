For parents
  • This story is Surah Yusuf (Qur’an 12:4–101), from the dream to the family reunited in Egypt. How the stories are made and checked is under “For parents” on the first page of Asmau.
  • Told more gently than the verses tell them: what the woman of the house tried to do (12:23–25), the gathering of the women of the city (12:30–32), and how the second prisoner’s dream ends (12:41), where the game says he “will be put to death”. Verses 12:52–53 are left out because scholars differ on who is speaking.
  • From Ibn Kathir’s tafsir and not the verses: the stone Yusuf stood on in the well, the shirt the brothers forgot to tear, the twenty coins, and the wind that carried the scent of the shirt.
  • Shown in this story: Prophet Yusuf and Prophet Yaqub (peace be upon them), Yusuf’s ten older brothers and his younger brother.
  • For ages 5 to 9. A child of 3 or 4 can enjoy it with a grown-up beside them: putting events in order, the picture puzzles and sorting need a helping hand, and the well, the prison and the second prisoner’s dream may need explaining or skipping.
