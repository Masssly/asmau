The man who bought Yusuf said: 'Look after him well! He may be useful to us, or we may adopt him as a son' (12:21). Ask: how should we treat someone new in our home or class?

The man who bought Yusuf was a minister of Egypt with the title al-'Aziz (Ibn Kathir). Ibn Mas'ud is reported to have said that three people showed the most insight: the 'Aziz, who told his wife to look after Yusuf; the young woman of Madyan who told her father to hire Musa (28:26); and Abu Bakr when he chose 'Umar to lead after him.

You may hear the woman of the house called Zulaykha. The Qur'an does not name her (12:23 calls her 'the woman in whose house he was living').
