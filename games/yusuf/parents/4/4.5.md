The verse ends: 'God always prevails in His purpose, though most people do not realize it' (12:21). Ask: can something that looks bad turn out to be good?

The commentators give names the Qur'an does not: the 'Aziz is Qitfir (Ibn Abbas, in al-'Awfi's report) or Itfir (Muhammad bin Ishaq); his wife Ra'il (Ibn Ishaq) or Zulaykha ('others said'); the king al-Rayyan bin al-Walid (Ibn Ishaq). All from Ibn Kathir's Arabic commentary on 12:21; none of the names is in the Qur'an.
