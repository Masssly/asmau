In Hayat al-Qulub, Imam al-Rida explains 12:24: the woman intended wrong and Yusuf intended to avoid it; the 'evidence of his Lord' was Allah's protection.

With young children you can say: someone tried to make Yusuf do something wrong, and he ran away from it.

You may hear what Yusuf 'saw' in that moment (12:24): some reports say the face of his father Ya'qub, others an angel, others a voice forbidding him. Al-Tabari, quoted by Ibn Kathir, says there is no clear proof for any one of these, so it is best left vague, 'as Allah left it'.
