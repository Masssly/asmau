Yusuf said: 'I shall manage them prudently and carefully' (12:55). Ask: what are you good at? What could you look after carefully at home?

You may hear that Yusuf later married the woman of the house. Ibn Kathir quotes Muhammad bin Ishaq: 'it was mentioned to me, and Allah knows best' that her husband died, and the king married her to Yusuf; 'they claim' she bore him two sons, Ephraim and Manasseh. Ibn Ishaq himself presents it as hearsay, and the Qur'an says nothing of it.

Ibn Kathir also quotes al-Fudayl bin 'Iyad: she stood by the road as Yusuf passed and said, 'Praise be to Allah, who makes servants into kings by their obedience, and kings into servants by their disobedience.' (Our translation from the Arabic.)
