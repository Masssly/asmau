For parents

What this is

Asmau is a small, free collection of Islamic games for children. Every game shows the ages it is for. A game marked “Coming soon” is not finished yet.

Where the stories come from

Stories from the Qur’an are checked against the verses and a named commentary, not written from memory. Inside each story, “For parents” says what was simplified for children, what was left out, and which details come from a commentary and not from the verses.

Your child’s privacy

There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. The site loads nothing from other companies, and nothing your child does is sent anywhere.

The voice

The stories are read by a plain computer voice until someone records them. You can record your own voice, and the games will use it. Your recordings stay on your device unless you choose to move them. You can also choose to keep them on all your devices with a family code; recordings are locked on your device first, and if you lose the code they cannot be recovered.

Without internet

After the first visit, everything works with no connection. On a phone or tablet you can add Asmau to the home screen from the browser’s menu.

The pictures

The pictures in the Yusuf story were made with an AI image generator. Everyone is shown with dark skin. The story’s own note for parents says who is shown, and why.

Supporting Asmau

Asmau is free, and you never have to pay or give anything to use it. It is kept going by donations from families who want to help. If you would like to, you can donate on Ko-fi. Donating does not unlock any game; the only thing it can do is raise your limit if you keep recordings online.
