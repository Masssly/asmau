Privacy note

Last updated 12 November 2026 (optional sync added). This note describes the site as it is today.

The short version

Asmau has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no cookies. It does not collect anything about you or your child.

What stays on your device

  • Where your child got to in a story, and whether sound and music are on.
  • Any voice recordings you make with “Record your voice”.
  • A copy of the site itself, so it works without internet.

All of this is kept in your browser on that device only. Asmau cannot see it. Clearing the browser’s site data removes it.

Voice recordings

A recording stays on your device unless you move it yourself: by saving it to a file, by sending it to another device with your phone’s own share menu, or by turning on the optional sync below. If you send a file through another app, that app’s privacy rules apply to it.

If you add a recording to the public copy of Asmau so that everyone hears it, that voice becomes public. Do not do this with a child’s voice without thinking it through.

Optional: keeping recordings on all your devices

You never have to use this. Without it, recordings work on the device they were made on and nothing is sent anywhere. If you choose it, this is what happens:

  • No account, no email, no password. You get a random family code (16 letters and numbers). That is the only thing that identifies you. We do not ask who you are.
  • Locked on your device first. Each recording is encrypted on your device, with a key made from your family code, before it is uploaded. The code itself is never sent. What is stored online is unreadable data: no one, including whoever runs Asmau, can listen to it or tell whose voice it is.
  • Where it is stored. On Cloudflare (a Worker and R2 storage), which holds the locked files under a name derived from your code. Cloudflare also sees technical details of a connection, such as the IP address, under its own privacy statement. Asmau does not receive or keep them.
  • What is stored. Only the locked recordings, their line names (for example “yusuf:c1-a”) and file sizes and upload times. Nothing else.
  • How long. Until you delete it. There is a “Delete my online copy” button that removes everything stored for your code. We do not yet delete old data automatically; if we add that, this note will say so first.

Keep your family code safe. We cannot recover it, and we cannot reset it, because we never have it. If you lose it, the recordings kept online can no longer be read by anyone and are gone for good. The recordings on your own devices are not affected. Anyone who has your code can read and delete your online recordings, so treat it like a password. A password manager is a good place to keep it.

This was chosen on purpose. A sign-in by email would be easier to recover, but would mean holding your email address and being able to reach your child’s voice. A code we cannot see protects your privacy more, and the price is that you must look after it.

Who hosts the site

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub can see technical details of a visit, such as the IP address, and handles them under its own privacy statement. Asmau does not receive this information.

Your rights and who to contact

Without the optional sync, Asmau holds no data about you. With it, the locked recordings are the only data, and we cannot link them to a person. You can delete them yourself at any time with “Delete my online copy”, which is your right to erasure under the GDPR. We cannot show you the contents of your recordings, because we cannot unlock them; you can download them on any device that has your code. Questions can be raised on the project’s GitHub page.
