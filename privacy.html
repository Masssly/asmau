Privacy note

Last updated 12 November 2026. This note describes the site as it is today.

The short version

Asmau has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no cookies. It does not collect anything about you or your child.

What stays on your device

  • Where your child got to in a story, and whether sound and music are on.
  • Any voice recordings you make with “Record your voice”.
  • A copy of the site itself, so it works without internet.

All of this is kept in your browser on that device only. Asmau cannot see it. Clearing the browser’s site data removes it.

Voice recordings

A recording never leaves your device unless you move it yourself: by saving it to a file, or by sending it to another device with your phone’s own share menu. If you send it through another app, that app’s privacy rules apply to it.

If you add a recording to the public copy of Asmau so that everyone hears it, that voice becomes public. Do not do this with a child’s voice without thinking it through.

Planned: keeping recordings on all your devices

We plan to offer an optional sign-in that keeps your recordings on all your devices. It is not available yet, and it will always be optional: recording on each device without signing in will keep working. Before it is switched on, this note will say what is stored, where, for how long, how it is protected, and how to delete it.

Who hosts the site

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub can see technical details of a visit, such as the IP address, and handles them under its own privacy statement. Asmau does not receive this information.

Your rights and who to contact

Because Asmau holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for it to show, correct or delete. If that changes, you will be able to ask for access to, correction of and deletion of your data, as the GDPR provides. Questions can be raised on the project’s GitHub page.
