# Adam: chapter outline (draft for approval)

Ages 5–9. Twelve chapters, about 60 pages and 30 activities. Sources per chapter; research in RESEARCH.md.
Where Sunni and Shia sources differ, the story uses words both accept and the parents' note gives both.

1. **Someone New** (2:30) — Allah tells the angels He will place a successor on earth; they ask why; "I know
   what you do not know." Activity: a question.
2. **Made from Earth** (15:26–29, 38:71–72, 3:59) — Allah gathers earth of every kind and colour (al-Tirmidhi and
   Abu Dawud; Imam Ali in Hayat al-Qulub), shapes Adam and breathes life into him. That is why people come in every
   colour. Activity: gather the earths; sort the colours.
3. **Al-hamdu lillah** — Adam sneezes and says al-hamdu lillah; Allah answers, "May Allah have mercy on you"
   (Sunni and Shia narrations). Activity: what do we say when we sneeze?
4. **As-salamu alaykum** — Adam greets the angels and they answer "wa alaykum as-salam wa rahmatullah": the greeting
   of his children (al-Bukhari; the greeting also in Hayat al-Qulub). Activity: greet them back.
5. **The Names** (2:31–33) — Allah teaches Adam the names of everything; the angels cannot say them; Adam can.
   Activities: match names to things; a memory game.
6. **The Bow and the Proud One** (2:34, 7:11–18, 15:30–42, 18:50, 38:73–85) — the angels bow; Iblis, one of the
   jinn made from fire, refuses: "I am better than him." He vows to whisper to people; Allah says he has no power
   over His true servants. Activity: sort proud and humble.
7. **The Garden** (2:35, 7:19, 20:117–119, 4:1) — Allah makes Hawwa for Adam; in the Garden no hunger, thirst or
   heat; eat anything, but do not go near one tree. Activities: gather fruit; find the tree.
8. **The Whisper** (7:20–22, 20:115, 20:120–121) — Iblis promises a tree of living for ever and swears he is a
   friend; Adam forgets, and they eat. They feel ashamed and cover themselves with leaves (pictures show leaves
   and the tree, not their bodies). Activity: do not listen to the whisper.
9. **Sorry** (7:23, 2:37, 20:122) — "Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves; if You do not forgive us and have mercy,
   we shall be lost." Allah, the Ever-Relenting, forgives them. Activity: put the prayer in order.
   Parents' note: the "words" of 2:37 in Sunni and Shia narrations; how each school sees the eating.
10. **Life on Earth** (2:36–38, 7:24–25) — they come down to earth, for a time; Allah promises guidance. It is told
   that Jibril taught Adam to farm and to pray, and that Adam and Hawwa found each other again near Mecca.
   Activities: plant and harvest; walk to find Hawwa.
11. **Two Brothers** (5:27–32) — each brother brings an offering; one is accepted; the other grows jealous and
   angry. The good brother says he will never raise his hand to hurt him. The angry brother killed him, and was
   sorry when he saw a raven bury another. Whoever saves one life, it is as if he saved all people (5:32).
   Told gently, with a note so parents can choose. Activity: choose kindness.
12. **Children of Adam** (7:26) — Allah gives Adam another son, Shith,
   who carries on his teaching (Sunni and Shia sources). We are all Adam's children, one family in every colour;
   the best garment is being mindful of Allah (7:26). Activity: tell the whole story in order.

Pictures: Adam and Hawwa with faces, Hawwa's hair covered; long simple garments in the Garden (7:27: they had
garments). Angels as tall beings of light with wings, faces lost in the light. Iblis as a dark shape of
smokeless flame, no clear face, not frightening. Earth scenes: a dry valley, then green fields.
Open choice: chapter 11 is in the Qur'an, but heavy for 5-year-olds.
