# Adam: research notes (Sunni and Shia sources)

Every claim here names its source. Items marked [unchecked] were seen only in a summary of a page and must
be read on the page before they go into the game.

## Qur'an (Abdel Haleem's translation, via quran.ai)
- 2:30-39, 7:11-27, 15:26-44, 17:61-65, 18:50, 20:115-123, 38:71-85, 3:59; Adam's two sons 5:27-32.
- The Garden had garments: Satan "stripping them of their garments" (7:27); in the Garden "you will never
  go hungry, feel naked, be thirsty, or suffer the heat of the sun" (20:118-119).
- Repentance words both traditions know: "Our Lord, we have wronged our souls: if You do not forgive us and
  have mercy, we shall be lost." (7:23)

## Sunni commentary (Ibn Kathir, abridged English, quran.com tafsir 169)
- Names: the names of everything people use (Ibn Abbas); the hadith of intercession (Bukhari, Muslim):
  "Allah ... taught you the names of everything".
- Hawwa: from Adam's rib, a report Ibn Ishaq takes from "the People of the Book and other scholars".
- The tree: unknown; al-Tabari: "We do not know which tree that was"; knowing it brings no benefit.
- The "words" of 2:37: most early scholars say 7:23. Ibn Abbas (al-Hakim, sahih chain): Adam asked, did You
  not make me with Your hands, breathe life into me, say "may Allah have mercy on you" when I sneezed ...
- Friday: Adam created, entered the Garden and left it on a Friday (Muslim).

## Shia commentary: al-Tabatabai, al-Mizan vol. 1 (English, al-islam.org)
Pages: .../surah-al-baqarah-verses-30-33, .../surah-al-baqarah-verse-34, .../surah-al-baqarah-verses-35-39
(https://al-islam.org/al-mizan-exegesis-quran-volume-1-sayyid-muhammad-husayn-tabatabai/...)
- The eating: "neither a sin (as this term is used in the Shari'ah) nor an injustice against Allah"; the
  command "did not have the force of an ordained law; it was only an advice", like telling someone not to put
  a hand in a fire. A wrong "against their own selves". No law had yet been given.
- 20:115 "he forgot": read as forgetting a covenant, not the prohibition.
- The Garden: "in the heaven, although it was not the Garden of eternal abode"; al-Qummi from Imam al-Sadiq:
  "a garden of this world, wherein the sun and the moon rose"; perhaps a Garden of al-Barzakh.
- The "words" of 2:37 (al-Kafi, from the fifth or sixth Imam): "There is no god except Thee; Glory be to
  Thee, O Allah! and praise! I have committed evil and been unjust to myself; therefore, forgive me".
  Another narration in al-Kafi: Adam asked by the right of Muhammad, Ali, Fatimah, al-Hasan and al-Husayn.
  A Sunni parallel in al-Suyuti's al-Durr al-Manthur: Adam asks "by the right of Muhammad", having seen
  "Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah" written on the Throne.
- Hawwa: the rib account is "a Biblical story which has been totally rejected by the Imams of Ahl al-Bayt";
  acceptable only if it means clay left over from Adam's.
- Descent: al-Qummi: Adam on al-Safa, Hawwa on al-Marwa; Adam "remained in prostration for forty days,
  weeping for the Garden". Other reports: Sarandib (al-Ihtijaj, from Ali), or Mecca.
- Tabatabai calls the serpent-and-peacock helpers of Iblis "extremely unreliable", taken from Jewish lore.
- The tree: 'Uyun Akhbar al-Rida: "a wheat-plant" and "the tree of envy"; he does not decide.
- [unchecked] Adam's clay lay forty days; the angels' remark; an angel friend; spikenard.

## How the game tells the differences
- Say what both accept: "Allah made Hawwa for Adam" (4:1, 7:189: "from it its mate"); "Adam forgot and ate";
  "they were sorry and said: Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves" (7:23); Allah forgave them.
- Parents' note: the rib (Sunni reports) or the same clay (Imams of Ahl al-Bayt); a sin or "leaving the
  better" / advice (Shia scholars, and many Sunni scholars on prophets' protection); the words of 2:37.
