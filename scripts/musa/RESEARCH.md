# Musa game: grounding notes

Working notes made while building the game. Every fact here was fetched with the quran.ai tools
(translation: M. A. S. Abdel Haleem; commentary: Ibn Kathir, abridged English), not recalled.
The game retells in simple words; it does not reproduce the translation.

## Translation, fetched 13 Nov 2026

### 28:3-43 (Al-Qasas): the backbone
- 28:4 Pharaoh made himself high, divided the people, oppressed one group, killing their sons, sparing their women.
- 28:5-6 Allah wished to favour the oppressed, make them leaders; show Pharaoh, Haman and their armies what they feared.
- 28:7 Mother inspired: nurse him; when you fear for him put him in the river; do not fear or grieve; We will return him and make him a messenger.
- 28:8-9 Pharaoh's household picked him up. Pharaoh's wife: "a joy to behold for me and for you. Do not kill him: he may be of use to us, or we may adopt him."
- 28:10-11 Mother's heart felt empty; Allah strengthened it. She told his sister "Follow him"; she watched from a distance, unnoticed.
- 28:12-13 He refused all wet nurses (ordained). Sister: "Shall I tell you of a household that could bring him up and take good care of him?" Restored to his mother, so she be comforted and know Allah's promise is true.
- 28:14 At full maturity: wisdom and knowledge.
- 28:15-17 Entered the city unnoticed; two men fighting, one of his people, one an enemy; his man cried for help; Musa struck the other with his fist and killed him. "This is Satan's work." "Lord, I have wronged myself. Forgive me" and He forgave him. "I shall never support those who do evil."
- 28:18-19 Next morning, fearful; same man cried for help again; Musa: "you are clearly a troublemaker." About to act, the man said "are you going to kill me as you killed that person yesterday?"
- 28:20-21 A man came running from the furthest part of the city: the authorities are talking of killing you, leave. Musa left, fearful: "My Lord, save me from people who do wrong."
- 28:22 Towards Madyan: "May my Lord guide me to the right way."
- 28:23-24 At Madyan's water: men watering flocks; two women holding theirs back: "we cannot water until the shepherds take their sheep away; our father is a very old man." He watered for them, withdrew to the shade: "My Lord, I am in dire need of whatever good You send me."
- 28:25-28 One came walking shyly: my father asks for you, to reward you. Told his story; old man: "Do not be afraid, you are safe now." Daughter: "Father, hire him: a strong, trustworthy man is the best to hire." Father: marry one of my daughters on condition you serve me eight years; ten if you choose. Musa agreed.
- 28:29-30 Term fulfilled, travelling with family; saw a fire on the side of the mountain; "Wait, I will bring news or a burning stick to warm yourselves." A voice from the right side of the valley, from a tree on blessed ground: "Musa, I am Allah, Lord of the Worlds."
- 28:31-32 Throw down your staff: moved like a snake; he fled; "Draw near, do not be afraid, you are safe." Hand inside shirt comes out white, unharmed. Two signs to Pharaoh and his chiefs.
- 28:33-35 "I killed one of their men, I fear they may kill me. My brother Harun is more eloquent: send him with me." "We shall strengthen you through your brother... they cannot touch you."
- 28:36-37 They said: conjuring tricks, never heard this from our forefathers.
- 28:38 Pharaoh: "you have no other god that I know of except me. Haman, light me a fire to bake clay bricks, then build me a tall building so that I may climb up to Musa's God: I am convinced he is lying."
- 28:39-40 Arrogant; seized and thrown into the sea.
- 28:43 Musa given the Scripture: insight, guidance, mercy.

### 20:9-48 (Ta-Ha): the fire and the mission
- 20:10 Saw a fire: "Stay here, I can see a fire. Maybe I can bring you a flaming brand or find guidance."
- 20:11-14 Called "Musa! I am your Lord. Take off your shoes: you are in the sacred valley of Tuwa. I have chosen you, so listen. I am Allah; there is no god but Me. Worship Me and keep up the prayer to remember Me."
- 20:17-21 "What is that in your right hand?" "My staff: I lean on it, restrain my sheep with it, and have other uses." Throw it: a fast-moving snake. "Pick it up without fear; We shall return it to its former state."
- 20:22-24 Hand under armpit comes out white, unharmed: another sign. "Go to Pharaoh, he has become a tyrant."
- 20:25-35 Musa's prayer: lift up my heart, ease my task, untie my tongue so they understand my words, give me a helper from my family, my brother Harun; strengthen me through him, let him share my task, so we glorify and remember You much.
- 20:36 "Your request is granted."
- 20:38-40 Flashback: mother inspired to put the child into the chest and the chest into the river; the river casts it on the bank; taken in by an enemy. "I showered you with My love... reared under My watchful eye." Sister: "I will tell you someone who will nurse him." Returned to mother. Later killed a man; saved from distress; years among the people of Madyan.
- 20:42-44 Go, you and your brother, with My signs. "Speak to him gently so that he may take heed, or show respect."
- 20:45-46 "We fear he will do us great harm." "Do not be afraid, I am with you both, hearing and seeing everything."
- 20:47 "We are your Lord's messengers, so send the Children of Israel with us and do not oppress them."

### 20:56-65: the contest is set
- 20:57-59 Pharaoh: you came to drive us from our land with sorcery; set an appointment. Musa: "the day of the feast, and let the people be assembled when the sun has risen high."
- 20:61 Musa warned the sorcerers: do not invent lies against Allah.
- 20:65 "Musa, will you throw first or shall we?"

### 20:66-73: the sorcerers
- 20:66-67 "You throw." Their ropes and staffs seemed to him to be moving (through sorcery); Musa was inwardly alarmed.
- 20:68-69 "Do not be afraid, you have the upper hand. Throw down what is in your right hand: it will swallow up what they produced: only a sorcerer's tricks."
- 20:70 The sorcerers threw themselves down: "We believe in the Lord of Harun and Musa."
- 20:71 Pharaoh: "How dare you believe before I give you permission?" and threatened terrible punishment (cut hands and feet, crucify). [Game: says only that he threatened them.]
- 20:72-73 "We shall never prefer you to the clear sign... decide whatever you will: you can only decide matters of this present life... Allah is better and more lasting."

### 20:77-79, 26:60-68, 10:90-92: the sea
- 20:77 "Go out at night with My servants and strike a dry path for them across the sea. Have no fear of being overtaken."
- 26:60-62 Pharaoh pursued at sunrise; when the two sides saw each other Musa's followers said "We shall definitely be caught." Musa: "No, my Lord is with me: He will guide me."
- 26:63 "Strike the sea with your staff." It parted, each side like a mighty mountain.
- 26:64-66 The others brought there; Musa and all his companions saved; the rest drowned.
- 10:90 As he was drowning Pharaoh cried "I believe there is no God except the one the Children of Israel believe in."
- 10:91-92 "Now? When you had always been a rebel!" "Today We shall save only your corpse as a sign to all posterity."

### 20:83-97: the calf
- 20:83-85 Musa came ahead in haste to please Allah; "We have tested your people in your absence: the Samiri has led them astray."
- 20:86 Musa returned angry and aggrieved.
- 20:87-88 People: we were burdened with the people's jewellery, threw it [into the fire], and the Samiri did the same; he produced a calf that made a lowing sound; "This is your god and Musa's god, but he has forgotten."
- 20:89 It gave no answer, no power to harm or benefit.
- 20:90-91 Harun had said: "this calf is a test for you. Your true Lord is the Lord of Mercy, follow me." They: "not until Musa returns."
- 20:92-94 Musa to Harun: what prevented you? Harun: "Son of my mother, let go of my beard and my hair! I was afraid you would say I caused division."
- 20:95-97 Samiri: "I saw something they did not..." Musa: "Get away from here!... we shall grind [the calf] down and scatter it into the sea."

### 2:57, 2:60: in the desert
- 2:57 Clouds for shade; manna and quails sent down: "Eat the good things We have provided."
- 2:60 Musa prayed for water; "Strike the rock with your staff." Twelve springs gushed out, and each group knew its drinking place.

### 7:130-137: the signs sent to Pharaoh's people
- 7:130 Years of drought and crop failure, so they might take heed.
- 7:131 Good came: "this is our due"; bad came: blamed Musa and those with him.
- 7:132 "We will not believe you, whatever signs you bring."
- 7:133 Flood, locusts, lice, frogs, blood: all clear signs.
- 7:134-135 Whenever a plague struck: "Musa, pray to your Lord for us: if you relieve us we will believe and let the Children of Israel go." When relieved, they broke their promise.
- 7:136-137 Drowned in the sea; the oppressed inherited the blessed land "because of their patience".

### 7:142-151: the mountain, the tablets, the calf
- 7:142 Thirty nights, then ten more: forty. Musa to Harun: "Take my place among my people: act rightly."
- 7:143 "My Lord, show Yourself to me." "You will never see Me, but look at that mountain: if it stays firm you will see Me." His Lord revealed Himself to the mountain and made it crumble; Musa fell unconscious; recovered: "Glory be to You! To You I turn in repentance! I am the first to believe!"
- 7:144-145 "I have raised you above other people by My messages and speaking to you: hold on to what I have given you; be one of those who give thanks." Everything inscribed in the Tablets.
- 7:148-149 In his absence the people worshipped a calf made from their jewellery that made a sound like a cow; it did not speak to them or guide them. Later they regretted.
- 7:150-151 Musa returned angry and aggrieved; threw the tablets down, seized his brother by the hair. Harun: "Son of my mother, these people overpowered me! They almost killed me!" Musa: "My Lord, forgive me and my brother."

### 2:67-73: the cow
- 2:67 "Allah commands you to sacrifice a cow." "Are you making fun of us?"
- 2:68 What sort? Neither too old nor too young, in between: "so do as you are commanded."
- 2:69 What colour? A bright yellow cow, pleasing to the eye.
- 2:70-71 Exactly which? Perfect, unblemished, not trained to till the earth or water the fields. "Now you have brought the truth." They slaughtered it, though they almost did not.
- 2:72-73 A person had been killed and they blamed one another; "Strike [the body] with a part of [the cow]": thus Allah brings the dead to life and shows His signs. [Game: tells the cow and the questions; the killing is in the parents' note only.]

### 5:20-26: the holy land
- 5:20-21 Remember Allah's blessing; "go into the holy land Allah has ordained for you; do not turn back."
- 5:22 "There is a fearsome people in this land. We will not go until they leave."
- 5:23 Two men whom Allah had blessed: "Go in through the gate... put your trust in Allah."
- 5:24 "You and your Lord go and fight; we will stay here."
- 5:25-26 Musa: "I have authority over no one but myself and my brother." "The land is forbidden to them for forty years: they will wander."

### 40:28 and 66:11
- 40:28 A secret believer from Pharaoh's family: "How can you kill a man just for saying 'My Lord is Allah'? He has brought you clear signs."
- 66:11 Pharaoh's wife prayed: "Lord, build me a house near You in the Garden. Save me from Pharaoh and his actions."

### 18:60-82: Musa and the man of knowledge (al-Khidr is the name from the commentary and hadith, not the verse)
- 18:60 Musa to his servant: "I will not rest until I reach the place where the two seas meet, even if it takes years."
- 18:61 There they forgot their fish, which made its way into the sea and swam away.
- 18:62-64 Further on: "Give us our lunch, this journey is tiring." Servant: "by the rock I forgot the fish; Satan made me forget; it made its way into the sea, how strange!" Musa: "That was the place we were looking for." They retraced their steps.
- 18:65 Found one of Allah's servants, given mercy and knowledge from Allah.
- 18:66-70 "May I follow you so you can teach me?" "You will not be able to bear with me patiently." "Allah willing you will find me patient." "Then do not ask about anything I do until I mention it myself."
- 18:71-73 In a boat: the man made a hole in it. Musa: "Do you want to drown its passengers?" "Did I not tell you...?" "Forgive me for forgetting."
- 18:74-76 They met a young boy and the man killed him; Musa objected strongly; "if I ask again, send me away." [Game: softened; parents' note says exactly what the verse says.]
- 18:77 A town refused them food; a wall about to fall; the man repaired it. Musa: "you could have taken payment."
- 18:78-82 "This is where we part." Boat: belonged to needy people; a king behind them was seizing every sound boat. Boy: his parents were people of faith; feared he would trouble them through wickedness; their Lord would give them a better child in his place. Wall: belonged to two young orphans; treasure buried beneath it; their father was righteous; Allah intended them to grow up and dig it out. "I did not do these things of my own accord."

## Commentary: Ibn Kathir's tafsir (abridged English), fetched 13 Nov 2026
Everything below is from the commentary, NOT from the verses. In the game each use is labelled on its scene and sourced in the parents' note.

### On 28:7-9 (quran.com/28:7/tafsirs/169)
- "It was mentioned" that Pharaoh's people feared losing their workers, so boys were killed one year and spared the next; Harun was born in a spared year, Musa in a killing year.
- Musa's mother's house was on the bank of the Nile; she made a box into a cradle; when someone she feared came she put him in the box in the river tied with a rope; one day she forgot to tie it and the water carried him past Pharaoh's house.
- Servant women picked the box up and took it to Pharaoh's wife unopened, afraid to open it without her.
- When opened: a child with the most beautiful features; Allah filled her heart with love for him.
- Her name: Asiyah bint Muzahim. She had no children from Pharaoh. Pharaoh wanted to kill the child; she said "a comfort of the eye for me and for you"; Pharaoh said: "For you he may be, but not for me."
- 20:39 "I endued you with love from Me": no one saw Musa but loved him.

### On 28:10-13 (quran.com/28:10/tafsirs/169)
- Mother's heart "empty": could think of nothing but Musa (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, Sa'id bin Jubayr, and others).
- The sister was older, of an age to understand. Ibn Abbas: she watched "off to the side"; Qatadah: looked as if not really interested.
- He refused all the wet nurses of the household, so they took him to the marketplace to look for one; the sister saw him there.
- Ibn Abbas: when she offered a household, they doubted her and seized her: "How do you know they will care for him?" She said: "because they want the king to be happy and hope for some reward." They let her go.
- The baby accepted his mother's milk; they rejoiced; Asiyah rewarded the mother, asked her to stay in the palace; she said she had a husband and children and would nurse him at home; Asiyah agreed and paid her.
- "There was only a short time between the distress and the way out, a day and night, or thereabouts, and Allah knows best."

### On 20:22-35 (quran.com/20:22/tafsirs/169)
- The hand "came out shining as if it were a half moon"; white "without any disease" (Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, Qatadah, Ad-Dahhak, As-Suddi); Al-Hasan al-Basri: "as if it were a lamp".
- "Untie my tongue": a lisp from an incident "when he was presented a date and a hot coal and he placed the coal on his tongue instead of the date" (the tafsir says the detailed story comes elsewhere). He asked only for enough to be understood.
- As a child Musa "stayed in Pharaoh's own room and slept on his bed."
- Ibn Abbas: Harun was made a prophet at the same moment as Musa. A'ishah heard a man say the brother most beneficial to his brother was Musa, who asked for prophethood for Harun; she said he spoke truthfully.

### On 28:21-24 (quran.com/28:21/tafsirs/169)
- Musa left Egypt alone; he had lived in luxury and was not used to being alone.
- "It was mentioned that Allah sent to him an angel riding a horse, who showed him the way. And Allah knows best."
- Madyan had a well where shepherds watered flocks. The women held their sheep back so they would not mix with the others'.
- "To the shade": he sat beneath a tree (Ibn Abbas, Ibn Mas'ud, As-Suddi). Ata bin As-Sa'ib: the women heard his prayer.

### On 28:25-28 (quran.com/28:25/tafsirs/169)
- The father was surprised the daughters came home so soon. Umar (chain graded sahih by Ibn Kathir): she came walking shyly, covering her face with her garment.
- Umar, Ibn Abbas, Shurayh, Abu Malik, Qatadah, Ibn Ishaq and others: the father asked "what do you know about that?" She said: he lifted a rock that only ten men could lift; and on the way back he asked her to walk behind him and throw a pebble to show the way.
- Ibn Mas'ud: three people had the best insight; one is the woman who said "hire him".
- Bukhari, from Sa'id bin Jubayr asking Ibn Abbas: Musa completed the longer term (ten years).

### On 26:60-68 (quran.com/26:60/tafsirs/169)
- They caught up at sunrise on the shore: sea ahead, Pharaoh behind. Harun was in front with Yusha' bin Nun and the believer of Pharaoh's family; Musa at the rear.
- "Like huge mountains" (Ibn Mas'ud, Ibn Abbas, Muhammad bin Ka'b, Ad-Dahhak, Qatadah). Ibn Abbas: the sea divided into twelve paths, one per tribe. As-Suddi: there were windows in the water so they could see one another; the water stood like walls. Allah sent wind to dry the sea bed.

### On 10:90-92 (quran.com/10:90/tafsirs/169)
- Same twelve paths and windows. Pharaoh was frightened at the sea and wanted to turn back.
- (Angels Jibril and Mika'il on horses in this account: NOT used in the game; angels are not depicted.)
- Ibn Abbas and others: some Israelites doubted Pharaoh was dead, so the sea cast his body, in his known armour, on high ground.
- Bukhari, from Ibn Abbas: the Prophet came to Madinah, found the Jews fasting Ashura, "the day Musa was victorious over Pharaoh", and said "You have more right to Musa than they, so fast it."

### On 7:130-135 (quran.com/7:132/tafsirs/169)
- Tufan: Ibn Abbas: heavy rain that ruined crops and fruit (also reported from him: mass death; Mujahid: water carrying plague). DISAGREEMENT noted.
- Locusts: Mujahid: ate the nails of their doors and left the wood. Ibn Ishaq: until doors fell.
- Qummal: Ibn Abbas: grain bug, or wingless small locusts; Al-Hasan, Sa'id bin Jubayr: small black insects. DISAGREEMENT noted. Sa'id bin Jubayr: ten bags to the mill gave three small bags.
- Frogs (Sa'id bin Jubayr): a person sat in frogs to the chin, could not open his mouth without one jumping in; Ibn Ishaq: frogs in houses, food, pots, clothes.
- Blood: rivers, wells and water containers turned to blood.
- Each time they begged Musa to pray, promised to believe and release the Israelites, and broke the promise (matches 7:134-135).

### On 2:60 (quran.com/2:60/tafsirs/169)
- Ibn Abbas: a square stone; twelve springs, three on each side; one per tribe.

### On 20:95-98 (quran.com/20:95/tafsirs/169)
- Ibn Abbas via Ibn Ishaq: the Samiri came from a people who worshipped cows and still loved it in his heart. Mujahid: the calf made a light moaning sound because wind blew through it.
- (The handful "from the track of the messenger" as Jibril's horse: NOT used; the game only says he threw something in.)

### On 18:60-65 (quran.com/18:60/tafsirs/169), hadith of Ubayy bin Ka'b in Bukhari as quoted by Ibn Kathir
- Musa was asked "who is the most learned?" and said "I am"; Allah told him of a servant at the meeting of the two seas who knew what he did not.
- "Take a fish in a basket; where you lose the fish, he is there." The servant boy is Yusha' bin Nun. They slept at a rock; the fish slipped out into the sea, and the water stood like an arch/tunnel over its path. Second narration: a spring at the rock called "Life" touched the fish.
- They found a man covered with a garment: al-Khidr (named in the hadith, not in the Qur'an).
- The boat crew knew al-Khidr and carried them for free. He pulled out a plank.
- A small bird perched on the edge of the boat and dipped its beak in the sea: "My knowledge and yours beside Allah's knowledge is like what this bird took from the sea."
- The Prophet said: "We wish that Musa had been patient, so that Allah would have told us more about them."
- (The boy in 18:74: the game softens this for small children; the parents' note says exactly what the verse says.)
