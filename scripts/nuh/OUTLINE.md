# Nuh: outline (for the owner's approval)

Eleven chapters, ages 5 to 9 (like Adam). Every chapter: verses on top, 4 to 6 narrated pages each with its own
picture, and 2 or 3 activities. Sources: scripts/nuh/RESEARCH.md.

1. **Forgetting Allah** (71:23, Ibn Abbas in al-Bukhari): long after Adam, people made statues to remember good
   men; later people forgot and began to worship them. *Activity: sort "Allah made it / people made it".*
2. **Nuh is sent** (11:25-26, 71:1-4, 29:14): Allah chose Nuh to tell them: worship Allah alone; He forgives.
   He kept calling them for 950 years. *Activity: count big numbers with stars (tap to fill).*
3. **Night and day** (71:5-9): he called them in the day and at night, out loud and quietly; they put their
   fingers in their ears. *Activity: day or night? sort the moments. Activity: a choice question.*
4. **Look at Allah's world** (71:10-20): rain, gardens, rivers, the sun a lamp, the moon a light, the wide earth.
   *Activity: tap the sky (sun, moon, clouds, rain). Activity: match each gift to what it gives.*
5. **Everyone is welcome** (11:27-31): the proud leaders sneered that only "the lowest" followed Nuh; Nuh would never
   send them away. *Activity: kind or unkind?*
6. **Build the Ark** (11:36-37, 54:13): Allah told Nuh how to build a ship of planks and nails.
   *Activity: hammer the nails (tap). Activity: build it piece by piece.*
7. **They laughed** (11:38-39): people passed by and laughed; Nuh kept building, patient. *Activity: hold the
   button to be patient, like the hold activity in Yusuf.*
8. **Two by two** (11:40, 23:27): water burst up from the ground, even where fires had burned; a pair of every
   kind, and the believers (about eighty, it is told). *Activity: find the pairs (memory). Activity: lead the
   animals aboard.*
9. **Bismillah** (11:41, 23:28-29): "In the name of Allah it sails and it anchors." *Activity: say the prayer in
   order, with the Arabic read aloud.*
10. **The big waves** (11:42-47), told gently: waves like mountains; Nuh called his son, who would not come; the
    waves came between them. Nuh asked Allah, and learned that family is also about doing what is right.
    *Activity: a choice question (no pictures of anyone in the water).*
11. **The humble mountain** (11:44, 11:48, 17:3, 71:28): "Earth, swallow your water; sky, hold back"; the Ark
    rested on al-Judi, the mountain that was humble (it is told, in both schools); "go down in peace"; Nuh, the
    thankful servant; his prayer for his parents. *Activity: drain the water (wipe). Activity: Nuh's prayer in
    order. Activity: tell the whole story in order.*

Left out: the dove, the raven and the rainbow (from the Bible, not our sources); Iblis on the Ark.
For the parents' notes: the son's name (Yam in Ibn Kathir, unnamed in Hayat al-Qulub), the "oven", how long the
building took, the Ark circling the Ka'ba (Shia narration), Nuh's wife (66:10).
