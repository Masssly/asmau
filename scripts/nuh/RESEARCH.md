# Nuh: research notes

Gathered 16 November 2026. Qur'an text and translation from quran.ai (Abdel Haleem's English; Uthmani Arabic);
the Sunni commentary is Ibn Kathir (abridged English, quran.com tafsir 169); the Shia source is Majlisi's
*Hayat al-Qulub*, vol. 1, "An Account of Nuh" (English, al-islam.org:
https://al-islam.org/hayat-al-qulub-vol-1-stories-prophets-muhammad-baqir-majlisi/account-nuh).

## Commentary: where the two schools agree (good for the story, "It is told that")

- **About eighty believers** on the Ark: Ibn Abbas in Ibn Kathir ("eighty people including their women");
  Imam al-Ridha in Hayat al-Qulub ("eighty believers").
- **Al-Judi, the humble mountain**: Mujahid in Ibn Kathir (the mountains rose up proudly; al-Judi humbled itself,
  so the Ark rested on it); Imam Musa al-Kazim in Hayat al-Qulub (al-Judi alone was humble, so it was chosen).
  A lovely shared lesson for children: Allah raises the humble.
- **"Under Our eyes, with Our revelation"**: Ibn Kathir: "We will teach you what to do." Both traditions say
  Nuh was taught how to build it.
- **Thankfulness**: 17:3 in the Qur'an; Imam Zayn al-Abidin (Hayat al-Qulub): people learned "thankfulness from Nuh".

## Commentary: only one school, or different (for the parents' notes)

- **The son's name**: Ibn Kathir names him Yam. Hayat al-Qulub does not name him. Both treat him as Nuh's own
  son; the Shia note explains "not of your family" as "not of the believing household" (those who obey Nuh are
  his family). The story says only "his son", which both accept.
- **The tannur ("oven")**: Ibn Kathir reports Ibn Abbas: the face of the earth; water gushed even from places
  of fire. Hayat al-Qulub places it in an oven in the Kufa mosque, where Shia narrations say the Ark was built.
  Story: "water burst up out of the ground, even where fires had burned" (holds both).
- **How long the building took**: Hayat al-Qulub: 30, 80 or 100 years in different narrations, and it rejects 500.
  Ibn Kathir gives no figure here. Story: "for a long, long time".
- **Size of the Ark**: Ibn Kathir quotes Muhammad bin Ishaq "from the Tawrah" (80 by 50 cubits, three decks:
  animals, people, birds). Israelite reports: leave out of the story; a parents' note at most.
- **First and last aboard**: "It has also been said" (Ibn Kathir) the parrot was the first bird and the donkey the
  last animal. Hayat al-Qulub tells the donkey with Iblis clinging to it. Leave Iblis out; the parrot is
  harmless "It is told".
- **The idols' origin**: al-Bukhari from Ibn Abbas (in Ibn Kathir): statues of righteous men, made to remember
  them; generations later, when knowledge was lost, people worshipped them. Hayat al-Qulub names other idols in
  Kufa, not the five Qur'anic names. Story: tells the al-Bukhari account gently, marked "It is told that".
- **Landing**: Hayat al-Qulub has the Ark circle the Ka'ba seven times before resting; dates differ (Rajab,
  Navroz). Not in Ibn Kathir's passage. Parents' note only.

## Left out, and why

- The dove with the olive leaf, the raven, and the rainbow: from the Bible, not from the Qur'an or these sources.
- Iblis on the Ark, and the vine shared with Iblis: unsettling for small children; parents' note at most.
- The wife's disbelief (66:10): told only in the parents' note.
- The drowning: told the way Adam's chapter 11 is: the waves came between Nuh and his son; the water covered the
  land; no pictures of anyone in the water.
