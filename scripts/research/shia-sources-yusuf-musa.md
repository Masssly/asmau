# What Shia sources add to the Yusuf and Musa games

Read for the existing games, to decide what could be added. Source: Majlisi, Hayat al-Qulub vol. 1 (English,
al-islam.org). The middle of the Yusuf chapter (prison to reunion) was only summarised by the reading tool and
is not used here. Each item names the narrator the book gives. Nothing here is in the games yet.

## Yusuf (https://al-islam.org/hayat-al-qulub-vol-1-stories-prophets-muhammad-baqir-majlisi/account-yaqub-and-yusuf)
1. Yusuf's prayer in the well (Ali ibn Ibrahim al-Qummi): "O the Lord of Ibrahim, Ishaq and Ya'qub. Have mercy
   on my helplessness, weakness, and tender age." Good for children; fits the well chapter.
2. Jibril comes to Yusuf in the well (Imam al-Baqir): Allah will make him "the Emperor of Egypt with honor and
   dignity" (the Qur'an itself, 12:15, says Allah revealed to him that he would one day tell them of this).
3. Why the family was tested (Abu Hamza al-Thumali from Imam Zayn al-Abidin): a fasting believer came to Ya'qub's
   door at iftar on a Friday and was sent away hungry; the Imam fed every beggar for fear of such a test.
   The translator notes the man's plea never reached Ya'qub himself. A lesson about feeding whoever comes to the
   door, but it blames a prophet's household: parents' note only, if at all.
4. Ya'qub on the unharmed shirt (al-Qummi, Imam al-Baqir): the wolf "tore apart Yusuf but did not damage his
   shirt a bit" (Sunni commentaries tell the same).
5. 12:24 (Imam al-Rida from Imam al-Sadiq): she intended wrong, Yusuf intended to avoid it; the "proof" was his
   protection by Allah. Fits how the game already tells it.
6. The bowing at the end was thanks to Allah (Imam al-Sadiq). Useful in the parents' note on the bow.
7. Names: his mother Rahil (Rachel), his brother Binyamin; Yusuf aged seven or nine at the well.
Not for children: the court scenes the book also tells, and its criticism of other readings.
