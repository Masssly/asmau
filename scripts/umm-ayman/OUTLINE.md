# Umm Ayman: outline (for the owner's approval)

Eleven chapters, ages 5 to 9, in "Great Muslims". Every chapter names its source on top (a verse where there is a
real one, otherwise the hadith or Ibn Sa'd), 4 to 6 narrated pages each with its own picture, and 2 or 3 activities.
Sources: scripts/umm-ayman/RESEARCH.md. Commentary-only things are marked "it is told".

**Who is shown (owner's decision, 16 November 2026: her face is shown).** Umm Ayman as she really was, an Abyssinian
woman; her sons Ayman and Usama, Zayd ibn Haritha, Anas as a boy, people of Makkah and Madina. **Never shown:** the
Prophet ﷺ at any age (not even as a baby or by his hands), his mother Amina, his grandfather, Khadija, Fatima,
'Ali, al-Hasan, al-Husayn, Abu Bakr and Umar. Their scenes are told through Umm Ayman's face, places and things (an
empty cradle with a soft light, two camels on a road, a doorway).

1. **Barakah from Abyssinia** (Muslim 1771; Ibn Sa'd): a girl from Abyssinia, across the sea, in the house of
   'Abdullah in Makkah; she was not free. *Activity: the journey across the sea (map board).*
2. **A baby is born** (93:6; Muslim 1771): his father died before he was born; Barakah looked after the baby with his
   mother. *Activity: a choice question. Activity: sing him to sleep (hold).*
3. **A month in Madina** (Ibn Sa'd): two camels, the uncles of Banu 'Adi; he learned to swim in their pond and flew
   pigeons, as he told later. *Activity: count the pigeons. Activity: find the pairs.*
4. **The road home** (93:6; Ibn Sa'd): his mother died at al-Abwa; Barakah brought the little boy home to Makkah on
   the two camels. Told gently. *Activity: lead the camels home (board).*
5. **"O blessed one"** (Ibn Sa'd): his grandfather: do not neglect this son of mine; when the grandfather died she saw
   the boy weeping; then his uncle Abu Talib. *Activity: who cared for him, in order.*
6. **Free, and a family** (Ibn Sa'd; Muslim 1771; Bukhari 3736): he freed her; Ayman, then Zayd and Usama; he called
   her "O mother"; a woman of the Garden (it is told). *Activity: match each person to who they are.*
7. **The long walk** (Ibn Sa'd): the hijra to Madina, fasting in the heat; it is told a bucket of water came down
   from the sky, and she was never thirsty again. *Activity: patience (hold). Activity: catch the water drops.*
8. **Water for the wounded** (Ibn Sa'd): at Uhud she carried water and cared for the hurt; at Khaybar too. No battle
   shown, only her care. *Activity: give everyone water (tap).*
9. **Ten palm trees** (Muslim 1771; Bukhari 2630; Muslim 2453; Ibn Sa'd): she spoke her mind; he joked kindly
   ("the baby of a she-camel"); he gave her ten times the palm trees. *Activity: count ten trees. Activity: the
   camel riddle.*
10. **Fatima's wedding** (Ibn Sa'd): she packed the bride's things: a bed of palm leaves, a leather pillow stuffed with
    fibre, a waterskin, soft sand on the floor. *Activity: pack the trousseau (sort).*
11. **The news of heaven** (Muslim 2454; 3:144): after he died, Abu Bakr and Umar visited her as he used to; she wept
    because the revelation had stopped, and they wept with her. *Activity: tell her whole story in order.*

For the parents' notes: what "not free" meant and how Islam encouraged freeing; the two dates of her death; her dream
before al-Husayn's birth (al-Amali, Shia; a similar dream of Umm al-Fadl in Sunni books); Fadak (al-Kafi, one plain
sentence); the bucket from the sky (al-Waqidi's chain, weak); Ayman, martyred at Hunayn.
Left out: Amina's fate (disputed), her words at Hunayn, "Peace, not on you", the lashing of the man who belittled her.
