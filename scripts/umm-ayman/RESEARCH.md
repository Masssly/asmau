# Umm Ayman (Barakah): sources

Every item below was read in the source itself (16 November 2026). Copies of the texts:
the scratchpad of that session; the references are enough to find them again.

## Hadith (Sahih Muslim, Sahih al-Bukhari)

- **Muslim 1771** (Book of Jihad; sunnah.com muslim:1771, in-book 32:84). Anas: the Prophet ﷺ gave Umm Ayman date
  palms; after Khaybar he returned them to Anas's mother "and gave to Umm Aiman instead of them date-palms from his
  orchard". Ibn Shihab adds: Umm Ayman, mother of Usama ibn Zayd, "was the slave-girl of 'Abdullah b. 'Abd-ul-Muttalib
  and hailed from Abyssinia. When Amina gave birth to the Messenger of Allah after the death of his father, Umm Aiman
  used to nurse him until he grew up. He (later on) freed her and married her to Zaid b. Haritha. She died five
  months after the death of the Messenger of Allah."
- **Muslim 1771 (second narration, in-book 32:85)** and **Bukhari 2630, 4120**: Anas came to ask for the palm trees
  back; Umm Ayman "put the cloth round my neck and said: No, by Allah, we will not give to you what he has granted to
  me." The Prophet ﷺ kept offering more "until he had granted her ten times or nearly ten times more".
- **Muslim 2453** (Merits of the Companions, in-book 44:147). Anas: the Prophet ﷺ visited Umm Ayman; she offered him
  a drink, he did not take it (perhaps he was fasting), "she raised her voice and showed annoyance to him".
- **Muslim 2454** (in-book 44:148). After the Prophet's death Abu Bakr said to Umar: "Let us visit Umm Aiman as
  Allah's Messenger used to visit her." She wept: "I weep because the revelation which came from the Heaven has
  ceased to come." "This moved both of them to tears and they began to weep along with her."
- **Bukhari 3736-3737**: Ibn Umar on al-Hajjaj ibn Ayman, "Aiman Ibn Um Aiman was Usama's brother from the maternal
  side"; the Prophet ﷺ loved the offspring of Umm Ayman.

## Ibn Sa'd, al-Tabaqat al-Kabir

Volume 1 (tr. S. Moinul Haq, Pakistan Historical Society; archive.org "kitab-al-tabaqat-al-kabir-ibn-sad-english-
translation"), mostly from al-Waqidi (weak in hadith terms; told as "it is told"):
- p. 109: his father 'Abdullah left Umm Ayman, five camels and some sheep; "Umm Ayman whose name was Barkah nursed him."
- p. 129: at six, Amina took him to her uncles of Banu 'Adi ibn al-Najjar in Madina, "and with him was his nurse Umm
  Ayman. They were riding two camels", staying a month. The Prophet ﷺ later remembered: "I used to play with Unaysah
  ... and I used to fly pigeons with the boys of my maternal uncles"; "I obtained proficiency in swimming in a pond of
  Bani 'Adi ibn al-Najjar." Amina died at al-Abwa on the way back; "Then Umm Ayman brought him to Makkah on the two
  camels which were with them."
- p. 74-75: 'Abd al-Muttalib to Umm Ayman: "O blessed one! do not neglect this son of mine."
- p. 75: at 'Abd al-Muttalib's death the boy was eight; "Umm Ayman said: I saw the Apostle of Allah weeping that day
  behind the bier of 'Abd al-Muttalib."
- p. 193: "Umm Ayman said: I never found the Prophet complaining against any young man or old, nor about hunger and thirst."
- Hijra: "Zayd Ibn Harithah brought his wife Umm Ayman with her son Usamah Ibn Zayd."

Volume 8, *The Women of Madina* (tr. Aisha Bewley, Ta-Ha 1995), pp. 156-159, "Umm Ayman, who is Baraka, the client and
nursemaid of the Prophet":
- inherited from his father with five camels and some sheep; freed "when he married Khadija"; married 'Ubayd ibn Zayd
  of the Khazraj, son Ayman (a Companion, martyred at Hunayn); then Zayd ibn Haritha, son Usama.
- He said to her "O mother", and when he looked at her he said "This is a remnant of the people of my House."
- Hijra (Uthman ibn al-Qasim): fasting and very thirsty at al-Munsarif below al-Rawha', "a bucket of water descended
  from heaven with a white feather"; she was never thirsty again, even fasting on a hot day.
- Sufyan ibn 'Uqba: "Whoever is happy to marry a woman of the Garden should marry Umm Ayman." So Zayd married her.
- The joke: she asked for a mount; "I will mount you on the offspring of the she-camel"; "All camels are the
  offspring of she-camels."
- Muhammad ibn 'Umar (al-Waqidi): at Uhud "she was bringing water and treating the wounded", and at Khaybar.
- Her weeping for "the news of heaven" when he died (Tariq ibn Shihab; Anas).
- Death: "at the beginning of the khalifate of 'Uthman" (al-Waqidi), against Muslim 1771 "five months after".
- Left out for children: "Peace, not on you" (her speech), her words at Hunayn ("You are harsh-tongued"), the qadi
  who had a man lashed for belittling her.
- p. 14-16 (Fatima): Umm Ayman at Fatima's wedding: "I arranged her trousseau ... a cushion of leather stuffed with
  fibre and soft sand was spread in her house"; the bed of palm leaves, the waterskin.

Ibn Kathir's Sira (Ibn Ishaq; al-Waqidi): Amina died at al-Abwa when he was six, returning from her uncles; Umm Ayman
was with them (hadithunlocked.com history:633). That page goes on to the question of Amina's fate: left out.

## Shia sources (thaqalayn.net)

- **al-Saduq, al-Amali, majlis 19 no. 1** (thaqalayn.net/hadith/29/1/19/1), from Imam al-Sadiq: Umm Ayman wept all
  night after a dream "as though one of your limbs was lying in my house"; the Prophet ﷺ: "Fatima will give birth to
  al-Husayn, and she will raise him." At the birth she wrapped the baby and brought him: "Welcome to the carrier and
  the one being carried." (Sunni collections tell a similar dream of Umm al-Fadl: check before using in a note.)
- **al-Amali, majlis 48 no. 3**: Umm Ayman wept that nothing was scattered at Fatima's wedding; the Prophet ﷺ told her
  of Paradise's gifts at it.
- **al-Kafi 1 (book of Hujja, 130:5)** and **Tahdhib al-Ahkam 4 (39:36)**: Fatima brought 'Ali and Umm Ayman as
  witnesses about Fadak. Sectarian; parents' note only, one plain sentence.
- **Muhsini, Mu'jam al-Ahadith al-Mu'tabara**: Umm Ayman "is from the inhabitants of Janna": the same as the Sunni
  "woman of the Garden".
