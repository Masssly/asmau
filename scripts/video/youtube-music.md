# The Asmau soundtrack on YouTube

Videos: scripts/video/music.js (one still picture per track, the 320 kbit/s MP3). Upload each as **Private** first, wait
for YouTube Studio's Checks (copyright) to finish, then set it to Public. Audience: **Yes, it's made for kids**. Add all
fifteen to a playlist called "Asmau: Music from the Games", in this order.

Description for every video (fill in the track name):

```
<Title> — from Asmau: Music from the Games.

The music of Asmau, free Islamic story games for curious kids. Every note is made by the games' own music engine: no samples, no borrowed melodies.
Play the games free at https://asmau.org — no adverts, no account, nothing to buy. Ever.

#IslamicKids #MuslimParents #Soundtrack
```
