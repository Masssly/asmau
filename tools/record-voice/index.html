Record Your Voice

Every game narrates its lines with the device's built-in voice, or a plain open-source placeholder, by default. Your own recorded voice always takes priority on whichever device you record it on — no code changes needed.

Recordings you make here are saved only in this browser, on this device — nothing is uploaded anywhere automatically. Press Download on a clip and add the file to the game's assets/audio/ folder in the repo (see the game's narration.json for the exact filename) if you want that recording to play for everyone who opens the game, not just here. Think before committing: once pushed, it's part of a public page.

Move recordings between devices. Save everything recorded on this device to one file, then load that file on another device (for example, record on your phone, load on the Fire tablet).

You do not have to do any of this: recordings work on the device they were made on, with nothing to set up. Signing in once to keep them on all your devices automatically is planned, and is not available yet. Privacy note
