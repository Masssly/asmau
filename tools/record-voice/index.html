Record Your Voice

Record your own voice for the stories. Your recording always takes priority over the computer voice, on the device you recorded it on. Read each line in whatever language your family speaks: the words on screen stay in English, but what your child hears can be yours. Record a few lines at a time; the rest keep the computer voice until you get to them. Recordings stay on this device unless you choose to send them somewhere (below). Privacy note

Want a recording to play for everyone?

Press Download on a clip and add the file to the game's assets/audio/ folder in the repository (see the game's narration.json for the exact filename). Think before committing: once pushed, it is part of a public page.

📲 Use my recordings on my phone and other devices

Record once and hear it on every device, with no file to move. This is optional. If you skip it, nothing changes and nothing leaves this device.

You get a family code instead of an account. Your recordings are locked with it on this device before they are sent, so nobody else, including whoever runs Asmau, can listen to them.

Keep your code safe. If you lose it, the recordings kept online are gone for good and cannot be recovered. You will still have the ones on your devices.

How: on the device that has your recordings, press Create my family code and save the code. Then open this same page on your phone, type the code under Already have a code?, and the recordings appear there.
